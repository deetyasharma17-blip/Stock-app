Investing Basics

What is a stock?

A stock represents ownership in a company. When you buy a stock, you own a small part of that company.

What is profit/loss?

Profit happens when the current price is higher than your buying price. Loss happens when it is lower.

What is risk?

Stock prices can go up and down. Investing all your money in one stock is risky.

Basic investing tips

Diversify your investments instead of putting all your money into one stock. Keep learning and invest for the long term.
